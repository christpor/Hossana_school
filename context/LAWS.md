## 📌 1. Absolute Foundation & Identity
- **Official Full Name**: Hosanna School for Cambodian Children (often referred to interchangeably as Hope School / 희망학교 in primary international charity documents).
- **Official MoEYS Recognition**: Recognized by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS) of Cambodia (*ក្រសួងអប់រំ យុវជន និង កីឡា*).
- **Core Brand Statement**: Built explicitly on what was once a notorious "slum area" in Phnom Penh to transform a poverty-stricken community into one built on education, generational recovery, and faith.
- **Operational Scale**: Over 800+ active students. Handles an entire academic pipeline from Early Childhood Education & Primary School through Upper Secondary High School (Grades 1 to 12).

## 💡 7. Financial Reality Strategy & Micro-Costs
- **The Core Strategy**: Public education in Cambodia technically lacks heavy baseline tuition, but localized day-to-day micro-costs (lesson printouts, clean water, uniforms, transportation) force children into manual labor dropouts.
- **Direct Action Tiers**:
  - **$15/mo**: Micro-supplies, printed workbooks, clean water supply, uniforms.
  - **$30/mo**: Full student pipeline sponsorship (learning materials, meal supplements, transport).
  - **$100/mo**: Extracurriculars, Hosanna Choir instruments, computer lab & print equipment.
